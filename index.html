How I sell a home

  1. Price it from real comparables

    A written valuation built from sales on your street in the last 90 days, not an algorithm.

  2. Prepare and stage

    A room-by-room list of small fixes, plus staging furniture from my own inventory at no cost to you.

  3. Photograph by day and at dusk

    Professional photos, a floor plan and a short video walkthrough, all booked and paid for by me.

  4. Launch for a full week

    A coming-soon campaign to my buyer list and local social media before the listing goes live.

  5. Negotiate and close

    I review every offer with you, handle the conditions and stay with you through closing day.
